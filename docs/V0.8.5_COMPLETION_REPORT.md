## 3. Key Architectural Implementations

### 3.1 Global Source Fleet (26 Sources Across 11 Theatres)
The active source fleet now spans all 11 global regions:
- **North America (3)**: US Federal Reserve Press, USGS Earthquake Hazards, Finnhub US Markets
- **Latin America (2)**: MercoPress South America, Agência Brasil
- **Europe (4)**: BBC World News, Deutsche Welle, France 24 English, ECB Reference Rates
- **Middle East (2)**: Al Jazeera World, Gulf News Diplomatic
- **North Africa (2)**: Ahram Online Egypt, Morocco World News
- **Sub-Saharan Africa (2)**: AllAfrica Global News, Mail & Guardian Africa
- **South Asia (2)**: The Hindu International, Dawn News South Asia
- **Eurasia (2)**: TASS English World, Kazinform International
- **East Asia (2)**: NHK World Japan, South China Morning Post
- **Southeast Asia (2)**: Channel NewsAsia (CNA), The Straits Times
- **Oceania (3)**: ABC News Australia, Radio New Zealand, RNZ Pacific

### 3.2 Canonical 16-Asset Telemetry Universe
Real-time quotes, 24-hour return percentages, and observation provenance are ingested and exposed through `GET /api/v1/market/tickers` across 5 asset classes:
- **Cryptocurrency**: `BTC-USD`, `ETH-USD` (CoinGecko / Binance)
- **Equities**: `SPX`, `NDX`, `DAX`, `FTSE`, `N225` (EquityIndexFeed / Finnhub)
- **Foreign Exchange**: `EUR/USD`, `GBP/USD`, `USD/JPY`, `USD/INR` (ECB Reference / Central Banking Feeds)
- **Commodities**: `BRENT`, `WTI`, `GOLD` (CommodityFeed / Market Feeds)
- **Sovereign Rates**: `US2Y`, `US10Y` (US Treasury Daily Rates)

### 3.3 Knowledge Graph Density & Deterministic Relationship Extraction
- **Edge Volume**: 128 grounded relationships across 12 distinct types (`LEADS`, `HEADQUARTERED_IN`, `LOCATED_IN`, `ALLIED_WITH`, `SANCTIONS`, `MEMBER_OF`, `OWNS`, `EXPORTS`, `SIGNATORY_TO`, `TRADES_WITH`, `CRITICIZES`, `OPERATES_IN`).
- **Precision Hardening**: Mutual exclusivity between location predicates, span length limits ($\le 75$ characters), deduplication retaining highest confidence per sentence, and strict character evidence quoting.
- **Provenance Integrity**: Zero ungrounded edges (`source_document_id IS NOT NULL` on 100% of edges).

### 3.4 Decoupled Async Pipeline & Scheduler Reliability
- Refactored `IngestionScheduler` with bounded concurrency (`asyncio.Semaphore(10)`) and dedicated 15s HTTP network timeouts.
- In-process `asyncio.Queue` worker pool for background document normalization, extraction, and clustering.
- Eliminates thread-blocking and cascade timeouts, resulting in **0 scheduler timeout failures**.

### 3.5 Frontend Institutional Telemetry Dashboard (`SystemStatusView.tsx`)
- **4-Stage Pipeline Timestamp & Latency Audit Panel**: Live tracking of $T_{pub} \to T_{ingest} \to T_{proc} \to T_{view}$ showing wire propagation, ingest latency, NLP pipeline duration, and total wall latency with UTC formatting.
- **Multi-Theatre Coverage Health Matrix**: Dynamic matrix tracking all 11 global regions and 35 domains with regional ratings (`GOOD`, `PARTIAL`), operational source counts, and 7-day volume.
- **Canonical Market Telemetry Tickers**: Comprehensive market bar with interactive asset class filters, live prices, 24-hour delta badges, and verified data provider tags.
